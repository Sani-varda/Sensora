Admission360™ Growth Engine

Built for schools, education groups, K-12 institutes, and education brands.

The commercial focus of educational marketing is not simply 'social media'; it is visibility, parent trust, qualified enquiries, and completed admissions. Strategic Difference

How does Admission360™ differ from generic school social media marketing agencies?

Traditional digital marketing agencies treat educational institutions as generic commercial accounts, focusing solely on aesthetic social graphics, holiday greetings, and vanity impressions that generate zero enrolled students. Sensora approaches education with commercial rigor focused on 4 core pillars: Visibility, Parent Trust, Enquiries, and Completed Admissions.

We build a complete enrolment engine: hyper-targeted Google search ads capturing active parents, branded video assets highlighting teacher pedagogy, automated WhatsApp parent journeys, tour booking funnels, and verified MIS enrollment attribution.

Enrolment Optimization

How does Sensora optimize the Lead-to-Visit and Visit-to-Application ratios for schools?

The biggest leakage in school admissions happens between an initial enquiry and the physical campus visit. Sensora solves this by implementing:

  • Instant WhatsApp Auto-Triage: Responds to parents within 3 minutes with digital prospectus highlights and interactive campus tour date pickers.
  • Frictionless Tour Scheduling: Parents can book, reschedule, or confirm weekend open-day walkthroughs with zero waiting.
  • Automated Nudge Sequences: Timely location maps, parking instructions, and curriculum overview packets delivered 24 hours and 2 hours prior to the campus visit.
School Heritage & Rebranding

Yes. Our School Brand Transformation honors your institutional heritage while modernizing your visual identity, positioning language, and admissions narrative to compete decisively with aggressive new international schools.
